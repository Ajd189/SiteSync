12 / FREQUENTLY ASKED

Answers before the first call.

How long does a project take?+

Timing depends on the package, content readiness, and platform access. We confirm milestones and responsibilities before work begins.

Who owns the domain and accounts?+

You do. Whenever possible, business-critical accounts are created in your name and remain under your control.

Do you need my passwords?+

Do not send passwords through this site. We use platform invitations, delegated access, or a secure handoff process when access is required.

What do revisions include?+

We agree on the content, pages, and revision scope before the build. Major redesigns or new custom features are quoted separately.

Are hosting and third-party costs included?+

No. Domain registration, hosting plans, premium apps, payment fees, ad spend, and other third-party subscriptions are separate.

What happens after launch?+

Your package determines the support period. You receive a handoff, and ongoing maintenance options are available when you need continued help.

How are payments handled?+

No payment is collected through this consultation form. Payment schedule and project terms are documented with the agreed scope.

Can you work with my current website?+

Yes. We can review what you already have, recommend what is worth keeping, and scope an improvement or rebuild.
